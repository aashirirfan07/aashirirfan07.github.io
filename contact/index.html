Questions before we start

What does a typical project cost?

Most marketing websites start from €6,000 and web applications from €15,000, depending on scope. After our first call I send a fixed quote, so there are no surprises later on.

Which technologies do you work with?

Mostly TypeScript, React and Next.js on the front end, with Node.js and PostgreSQL behind it. If your team already has a stack, I am happy to work within it.

Can you build from our designer's files?

Yes. Around half of my projects start from another designer's work. I join early to flag anything tricky to build and keep the result faithful to the design, down to the last pixel.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a few weeks of support, documentation and a walkthrough for your team. After that, many clients keep me on a small monthly retainer for updates and improvements.
