Overview

Students upload files, set colour, duplex, paper and binding options, see a live price estimate and choose a campus print station. Each order gets a time-limited QR code and a 4-digit PIN for pickup, while shop operators work from a live job queue with a PIN keypad and analytics, and a role switcher demos both sides.
